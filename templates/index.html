How To Spot Generative Media

Learn the subtle physical and acoustic anomalies that generative models struggle to simulate.

📸

Synthetic Images

  • Pupil Catchlights: Real camera lights reflect identical shapes in both eyes; AI often produces mismatched reflection geometries.
  • Ear & Teeth Cartilage: Watch for blurred, merged earlobes or teeth that blend into solid surfaces without distinct dental margins.
  • Vanishing Perspectives: Background windows, balustrades, and structural lines frequently bend or fail to meet at coherent vanishing points.
🎙️

Cloned Voice & Audio

  • Breath Micro-Dynamics: Human speakers naturally inhale, pause, and exhale between clauses; neural TTS often speaks without breathing.
  • Metallic Vocoder Timbre: Higher vocal frequencies often exhibit subtle robotic buzzing, digital quantization, or unnatural pitch flatness.
  • Room Acoustics: Authentic audio contains natural room reverberation; synthetic clips often sound artificially dry and isolated.
🎬

Generative Deepfake Video

  • Temporal Consistency: AI video generators struggle with object permanence; background objects often morph frame-by-frame.
  • Physical Momentum: Look at hair motion, water droplets, and vehicle wheel rotation to see if Newtonian inertia is physically respected.
  • Facial Boundary Warping: Watch the jawline, neck transitions, and hairline during rapid head rotation for subtle flickering artifacts.
